# Changelog

## 1.4.0 (25 September 2026)
- **A brand-new first five minutes.** Sensei asks your child (out loud, with big pictures) whether they go to school yet and which class, and starts them in the right place. Grown-ups can change it any time in the grown-ups' settings. If the first questions are too hard, the game quietly moves back a step.
- **Six short warm-up games before letters**, made for 3- and 4-year-olds: Ninja Ears (fast and slow words with a tortoise and a rabbit, "words are made of sounds", hearing the first sound), Ninjas Read This Way (reading pictures left to right: a fish and a dog make a fish-dog!), picture words (sun + flower, rain + bow, snow + man), turning sounds into words, and sound dots. Each game starts with "Let me show you!", then "Now you try!", and the first lesson takes about a minute and a half, the second about a minute.
- **The Sticker Book.** Every picture your child plays with becomes a sticker, with a big celebration after the first games (and a shiny fish-dog). Words they read or spell become gold stickers.
- **Clearer, friendlier pictures** on coloured cards: animals and people always have faces, nothing is cut off, and Sensei lights up each picture as she names it, in whole, smooth sentences.
- **The World Flower comes alive.** Winning a gem plays a victory to swelling music as the gem flies into its petal. Tap a petal to hear the words your child has found with that sound, and tap the green gate to practise a spelling in a short dojo. After learning new spellings, and at the start of each new land, Sensei brings your child to the flower to explain what they've found.
- **Sensei explains things at the right moment,** in Sounds~Write teacher language: what the dojo is, reading "this way" (left to right), first, middle and last sounds, "It's two letters, but it's one sound", when one spelling can be two sounds (thin, this), sounds that sit next to each other (frog, tent), common words in stories ("This is 'the'. Just say 'the' here."), the gem battle's timer, and what a streak is. Long explanations aren't repeated every level, and corrections vary instead of saying the same sentence again and again.
- **Help never sounds out a word your child is spelling.** Sensei says the whole word slowly and asks "What sound do you hear here?", as Sounds~Write teachers do.
- **The order of learning follows Sounds~Write more closely.** "Same sound, different spellings" sorting now comes after all the single sounds (the Bridging Unit), and the Sky Temple's long-vowel spellings come in the official order.
- For children starting at school level, the first two lessons are short (about 90 and 60 seconds) and always finish at the end of a word.
- Fix: the words on story pages no longer pile up on top of each other.
- A gentler start for the youngest ninjas. Bamboo Village now begins with sounds only (listening games and "which one starts with /s/?"), then shows how each sound is written, then builds two-sound words before three-sound words. Sensei shows how first ("I do"), you try together ("we do"), then you have a go ("you do").
- Sensei stretches words slowly ("mmmaaat") so children can hear every sound.
- "Who read it right?": Kai and Suki each read a word, and children spot who got it right.
- Sensei Maple is now a kind grandmother red panda with spectacles and a silver bun, and her mouth moves with what she says.
- The opening film is re-cut so the pictures match the words, and the pandas match the rest of the game.
- Grown-ups can jump ahead to "end of Reception", "end of Year 1" or "end of Year 2".
- The Sound Flower and Word Book are introduced step by step, in words little children know.
- The story now centres on the **World Flower**: a magical flower of 44 rainbow sound petals at the heart of the island. Baron Muddle blows it apart, and every petal a child wins helps it bloom again.
- A new 8-shot opening film: the island thriving, Baron's storm, petals scattered across the lands, frightened islanders, and Sensei calling on the Super Ninjas.
- The World Flower screen is now a swipeable ninja scroll with big petals. Sounds not yet met are hidden in mist, gems glow as they're won, and a vine shows how far you've come.
- A new trailer, which is also the video shown when the site is shared.
- Fixes: letters no longer wrap and overlap in "find the sound", the "how we write it" letter is big and clear, clearer pictures for pin, sand and soap, and a stronger glow when Sensei points at the answer.

## 1.2.0 (25 September 2026)
- Player profiles: each child types their name and gets their own saved progress.
- A new "Show Sensei" starting-level game that asks the questions teachers ask: which spelling makes this sound, which word is it (with near-identical choices), and which spelling of a sound is in a word.
- Baron Muddle is now a proper villain, with a menacing new look, voice lines and intro. When he speaks, his portrait storms onto the screen.
- The Sound Flower now looks like the school's petal chart: every sound is a teardrop petal with its spellings inside, and each spelling is a gem.
- A "Get ready" page helps grown-ups go full screen or add the game to the home screen.
- The website has sound, and its gameplay videos have real narration, music and effects.
- Early "same sound, different spelling" levels (c/k, l/ll, s/ss, c/k/ck, ch/tch).
- If anything goes wrong, a friendly screen offers "Try again" instead of a blank page.

## 1.0.0 (25 September 2026)
The first public version.
- Six lands and 54 levels, from single sounds (a, i, m, s, t) to long vowel spellings (ai/ay, ee/ea, igh/ie, oa/ow).
- Game modes: Dojo, Spell Battles, Ninja Run, Sound Swap, Sorting, illustrated Stories and Boss Battles.
- The Sound Flower: 44 petals (one per sound), each holding gems for its spellings. Practice charges a gem, and a timed Gem Trial wins it.
- Ninja Training (a controls tutorial), "Show Sensei" (placement at the right starting level), and Sensei's help button on every screen.
- The Word Book: a sticker scrapbook of every word learned.
- A landing page with gameplay videos and screenshots.
