Speech templates · pilot

Jonas, 27 Sep: “instead of ‘say this sound: a’ you can say ‘say the sound a’ … instead of ‘say this word slowly: mat’, you would say ‘say mat slowly’.” Each pair is the same moment in the game said two ways: today (the recorded carrier line, then the word or sound clip, as the game joins them now) and new (the template: the word said inside one recorded take, or a lead-in with the checked pure sound after it). Both are Erinome. Units IC1–IC4 (the first two worlds). Blind mode hides which is which.

75%without the two same-words controls (108 votes)

What the pilot found

  • The judge preferred the template in 81 of 108 votes (75%): word templates 39 of 51 (76%), sound templates 42 of 57 (74%). The two same-words controls split 6 of 12, so it isn't just picking the new recording. 30 of the 40 pairs went to the template. (Re-judged 28 Sep after the re-render; votes on pairs whose audio changed were cast again.)
  • Jonas's examples: “Say the sound… /a/” won 9 of 9 against “Say… /a/”. “Say mat slowly.” won 7 of 9 against “Say this word slowly… mat” (a carrier recorded for this pilot: the game has no such line). It took 5.4 takes a word to get there, and the TTS quota ran out before the last 47 of its 85 words; those 48 were rendered on 28 Sep (4.6 takes a word, all 85 now recorded). With the new A/B samples w_say_slowly won 5 of 9.
  • Where the template lost: w_listen_again 2 of 9. Its takes are rushed (3.4–3.8 words a second), because the generator ranks the take with no pause above the one at a teacher's pace, and the judge hears the word as dropped in. ws_way_we_spell 3 of 9: a pure sound in the middle of the sentence (“…spell /b/ …in bat.”) loses to today's order, the sound first and then “This is the way we spell it in bat.”
  • Joins: today the pause before a pure sound is 278–304 ms. The template's is 150 ms. Under the edge rule fix-requests.md gives audio.ts (40 dB under the clip's peak), 14 of 408 joins came out 25–180 ms long, because 39 of 151 lead-ins end in a faint tail. With the stricter of that rule and −45 dBFS, 1 join is out (the page plays it this way). The 9 lead-ins that were out of range on timing or level were re-rendered on 28 Sep: 7 are now in range; ws_starts_with “Man starts with…” (+4.1 dB after the gain table) and “Sausage starts with…” (+3.3 dB) are still just over on level.
  • Level: a pure sound sits a median +1 to +2 dB over the last half-second of the new lead-ins. After today's lines it sits +4 to +14 dB over. The exceptions are s_here_sound's lead-in, now in range after its 28 Sep re-render, and the read-back's tiles, at +5 dB.
  • Render: 517 of 591 pieces were recorded (IC1–IC4, all 16 templates) in 106 minutes: 1,554 TTS takes and 217 judge calls. 39 pieces failed every take. 12 of those are Whisper spelling the word as a name (Finn, Cobb, Kat, Kot, Kan, Anne). 10 are ww_change takes read too fast (3.4–4.8 words a second). 12 hit the key's TTS limit of 10,000 requests a day, which the pilot shares with other workflows. 35 pieces were never reached. On 28 Sep regen.sh rendered 73 more pieces in 14 minutes (318 takes): 573 of 591 are now recorded. 18 still fail: the 10 ww_change pieces (left for the template split) and 8 Whisper misspellings (Kat, Kot, Anne, Cobb, Finn, “Indian”, “CART”) plus one judge fail on “Your word is fin.”
