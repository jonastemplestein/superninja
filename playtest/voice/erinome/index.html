Sensei's new voice: Erinome

Your pick from the voice picker ("the erinome voice is clearly the winner"). Sensei teaches, and she narrates the stories and the opening film too. The Baron keeps Algenib.

Every Sensei clip in the game is now Erinome: 1,366 lines, 65 story clips, 1,255 words, 7 held first sounds, the 46 pure sounds and the 1,015 slow words made from them.

What changed

  • Sensei's voice changed from Sulafat to Erinome (Gemini TTS, en-GB). She also narrates the stories and the opening film. The Baron stays Algenib: 14 of his 17 lines are untouched, and the 3 newest were recorded in Algenib.
  • Re-recorded with the same words and ids: 1,366 lines, 65 story clips (pages and titles), 1,255 words and 7 held first sounds ("Sssun").
  • The 46 pure sounds were rebuilt from Erinome recordings, with your Sulafat lessons as rules. A stop is its burst only (no "duh"). A short vowel is 0.15–0.25 s (no "or" or "ar"). Nothing unvoiced is time-stretched: /h/ is a natural 0.2 s cut, and /th/ is lengthened with noise grains. /r/ is a natural 0.21 s cut. Only /l/ and /z/ are lengthened, with WORLD. No letter names.
  • The 1,015 slow words were re-made from the new pure sounds. Every timing table (clip lengths, slow-word onsets, word spotlights) was re-measured on the new takes.
  • The old Sulafat clips are kept in .trash/sulafat-2026-09-27.

How it was checked

  • No Sulafat left. Every Sensei clip is newer than the switch. None is byte-identical to any older file, and none is the same recording as its Sulafat clip (waveform correlation at most 0.73, and only on short words). A voice-print spot check put 10 of 10 random clips with Erinome, and 16 of 16 Sulafat controls with Sulafat. The slow words are made of the Erinome sounds: none of 3,351 sound stretches matches a Sulafat sound.
  • Loudness and pace on every clip. 2,686 of 2,686 are at −16 LUFS (−19 under 1.2 s), ±1. No line is over 3.3 words a second. The 182 lead-ins all end cleanly.
  • Whisper on a random 60 lines, 10 story pages and 40 words: each says its text, give or take homophones and spellings ("practised", "flu", "Cobb").
  • Accent (the calibrated judge) on the 36 sampled clips with a BATH or r word: 32 British (89%). On 24 more lines whose r words the gate's trigger list skips: 23 British. Rechecked on 21 votes: "Ninja power!" (4/21) and "Here's another way..." (0/21, where the Sulafat take was 21/21) are clearly not British, and two BATH lines sit just under the gate (15/21). Those four are first in "For your ear", to retake.
  • Pure sounds. 46 of 46 pass the acoustic gate and the length rules. The blind padded judge called 40 of 46 clean (the /oe/ and /ue/ "letter names" are those sounds).
  • Warmth. Gemini gave 8.6/10 over 10 lines, British on every one. (It also called a Sulafat control "the same speaker", so it can't tell the two voices apart.)
  • In the game. A frozen build, played by two bots through the first minutes and 4 levels: no missing clips, no page errors, and no clip cut short or overlapped because of the new lengths. durations.json matches every clip to the millisecond.
