Before you pick a new Sensei

A new Sensei voice means re-recording everything she says, and rebuilding the pure sounds from her voice:

  • about 1,250 lines and the 65 story pages,
  • about 1,240 words, plus the stretched and held words,
  • all 46 pure sounds, which today are cut from Sulafat’s words.

That’s a few hours of automated work, then a listening check. A separate narrator only re-records the story pages and the film’s narration. A new Baron re-records just his 14 lines.

Whichever voices win, every new take gets the accent check and is re-done until it’s clean.

How to listen: the gold lines test the accent. Use headphones if you can. Blind mode hides the names and scores, so you judge by ear.

How the scores work

British (out of 10)
A listening judge hears each clip next to a known British voice and a known American voice saying the same words, and says whose accent it shares. Acoustic measurements check the r in “car” and “four”, the “ah” in “bath”, “grass” and “fast”, and the t in “water” and “butter”, over up to three takes. 8.5 or more is British, 7 to 8.5 mostly British, under 5 American. A plain “is this British?” judge isn’t used: it called an American voice British 15 times out of 18.
Warmth, storytelling, villainy
A Gemini judge scores each voice out of 10 for its role: a warm Reception teacher for Sensei, a bedtime storyteller for the narrator, a funny pantomime villain (never frightening) for the Baron. Each provider was judged in its own run, so treat small differences loosely.
Takes (t2, t3)
Extra takes of the accent lines. The accent changes from take to take, so a voice that stays British on every take is the safer choice. A red dot marks a take where the measurements found an American sound.
Top 3 per provider
Each provider’s own shortlist for the role. OpenAI’s gpt-audio-1.5 sounds British; its gpt-4o-mini-tts is mostly American even when asked for British English, so “British only” hides most of those.
Price
US dollars per minute of finished speech. Sensei’s whole library is roughly 46,000 characters of lines, plus the words and story pages.
