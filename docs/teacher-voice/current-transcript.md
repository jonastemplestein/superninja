# The journey, turn by turn: full transcript

The companion to [current.md](current.md): every Sensei turn and every child tap, for the three listener runs of 26 Sep (a brand-new preschool child from the title to w2-1; w2-1 on the production build; the first sort). One row per Sensei turn: every clip said between two child actions, verbatim, with spliced sounds and words in brackets ([/s/] a pure sound, ["sun", slowly] a stretched word, ["sun", first sound held] a held first sound; ✂ a clip cut off by the next). Times are game seconds from the start of each moment. The last column is the DOM at the turn's first line: scene/beat, what can be tapped, and the nav row. Line ids are in `src/content/lines.ts`.
