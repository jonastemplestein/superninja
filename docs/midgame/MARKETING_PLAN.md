# Marketing plan: showing Year 1 and Year 2, honestly

**27 September 2026.** How the website (`index.html`, `landing/`) and the videos show Super Ninja to parents of children in Year 1 and Year 2, and prove it, without claiming anything the game can't do on the day the claim goes up. It follows the design in [../MIDGAME_ENDGAME.md](../MIDGAME_ENDGAME.md) (revision 2) and the slices in [BUILD_PLAN.md](BUILD_PLAN.md).

Jonas, 27 September: "in your marketing materials on the website and in the videos you render for me … you need to be way more focused on making sure this all makes sense as well for kids in year 1 and year 2 … And just like prove it with the marketing materials updated to reflect that."

**[R3: Jonas, Baron final only]** Jonas, the same evening: "I don't really understand one thing, which is how we have a video of a battle with Baron Muddle, with the final super boss, with like super simple words in it. That should be the final, final battle with really hard words always." The design now has the Baron fight only once, in the final battle (spec §R.3), and §4.1 lists every page and video that shows him fighting over simple words, with what changes when the interim swap ships.

**The one rule: a claim ships with the slice that makes it true.** Every Year 1–2 claim below names the slice it waits for, and the clip that proves it. Until a slice ships, the site says what is playable today and what is being built, in plain words.

## 0. The short version

- **Today the game fits ages 3 to about 5½.** It reaches the first half-term of Year 1 (audit §0). The site's "aged 3–8", "44 sounds, every spelling" and "Complete it, and you've cracked the code of English" overclaim, and it still shows < a‑e >, which the game dropped. Stage A (§2) fixes that now, before any new slice.
- **A new section, "From first sounds to *multiplication*"** (§3), carries Years 1 and 2: the four maps and the Library as a road, with a badge on each saying what is playable now; how long words are taught; how bosses work; and, as they ship, the weekly list and the school start. The road is honest from day one because its badges say where the game is.
- **What each slice lets us claim** (§1): after Slice 1a, *sunset* built as a Word Dragon (Year 1) and *station* as a labelled "Year 2 preview"; after 1b, a Year 1 boss that tests spelling choice; after Slice 2, a whole Year 1 land; after Slice 3, "your school's weekly list, in one link"; after Slice 4, "already at school? start where your class is"; land by land after that; *multiplication* in the hero only when the Muddle Isles ship.
- **[R3: Jonas, Baron final only]** **No Baron fight over simple words, anywhere** (§4.1): the trailer's "THE FINAL SHOWDOWN" (*beg*), the enemy supercut's last block (*coat*, *tea*), the clips page's `boss-3` and the landing's boss copy change when the interim swap ships. The only Baron fight the site will ever show is the final battle's (clip 18).
- **Clips** (§4): 19, each recorded on production with the real game, at a child's pace, with one mistake so the correction shows; none before its slice passes acceptance.
- **The copy** (§5) is in Jonas's voice: a dad building a phonics game for his kids, direct and specific, with real words from the game.

### 5.1 The section's title and lede (Stage A)

### 5.2 Blocks, as they ship

**Long words, the school way** (Slice 1a):

> **Long words, the way school teaches them.** In Year 1, children start building two-syllable words like *sunset*. Sensei says the word and how many syllables it has, your child marks where they join, builds each syllable sound by sound, reads it slowly, then reads the whole word fast. When the syllables join up, the word hatches into a dragon. That's Sounds~Write's Lessons 11 to 14, with tiles instead of a whiteboard.
>
> *What about* magician *and* division*?* -tion (*station*) is Year 2. -cian and -sion (*magician, division*) are Year 3 at school, so Super Ninja teaches them after Year 2, with *magic* as the clue for *magician*. You can try a Year 2 preview, *station*, from the grown-ups page.

**Bosses that check** (Slice 1b):

> **How the boss battles work.** Is the panda the boss of lame sounds? It's the boss of the first eight sounds, which is the hardest thing a four-year-old has ever done with letters. Every boss after it is its land's progress check, like the ones school gives every six to eight weeks: it reads, it spells, it throws a long word at you, and you finish it with a sentence. So the bosses get harder because the sounds do. The Sky Magpie steals the vowel out of *r _ n*, and your child has to know which spelling goes back. **[R3: Jonas, Baron final only]** And Baron Muddle himself fights only once, in the very last battle, with the hardest words in the game.

**Your school's weekly list** (Slice 3):

> **This week's spelling list, in one link.** Every week, Year 1 and Year 2 children bring home a list: a sound with its different spellings, and a couple of special words. Paste the list into Super Ninja (or tap your school's link) and your child gets five minutes a day on exactly those words, and a practice test the day before school's. Special words are taught the Sounds~Write way: Sensei gives the bit your child hasn't learned yet, and they build the rest. The list stays on your device. No accounts.

**Already at school?** (Slice 4):

> **Already at school? Start where your class is.** Tell Sensei which year your child is in and they start about half a term behind their class, so it's practice, not a race. We assume they know the earlier sounds, and they collect them as they show them. If there's a gap, a magic door appears on their path and takes them back for a few minutes. **What age is it for?**

## 6. Checks before anything goes live

- **The claim audit.** Before each landing release, a script (`scripts/landing-claims.ts`, new, in the release checklist) lists every claim line on the page with the slice it needs (a `data-needs="1b"` attribute on each block), and fails the release if a block's slice isn't in the build (`worlds.ts` has no `w6-syl1`, say).
- **The badge data** comes from the build, never by hand.
- **Every clip** is re-watched against its slice's acceptance: the pure-win knockout before the chest (clip 3), no "special words" said to the child (clip 8), the "Year 2 preview" label on clip 2.
- **The parent persona** (PLAYTEST_PLAN §2) reads the page on a phone and answers: "What will my Year 1 child get today? What isn't built yet?" Its answers must match the road's badges.
